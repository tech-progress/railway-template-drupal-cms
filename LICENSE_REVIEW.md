# License review (2026-11-24)

- **Drupal core.** `core/LICENSE.txt` in `drupal/drupal` at `11.4.13` is the GNU GPL version 2 text; Drupal is distributed as GPL-2.0-or-later. Redistribution, including commercially, is allowed with source available. The template redistributes unmodified Drupal core, contributed modules and Drush, built from the public `composer.lock`. Its own changes (settings file, start wrapper, Apache and PHP settings) are published in the template repository.
- **Drupal CMS.** `drupal/cms` `composer.json` at `2.2.10` declares `GPL-2.0-or-later`, and `LICENSE.txt` (copied to `site/LICENSE.txt`) is the GPL version 2 text. The contributed modules and recipes it pulls are hosted on drupal.org, which accepts only GPL-2.0-or-later compatible code. Their JavaScript and other libraries are GPL-compatible.
- **Drush.** GPL-2.0-or-later.
- **PostgreSQL.** PostgreSQL License (permissive); the official image runs unmodified.
- **Trademark.** Drupal's trademark policy (https://www.drupal.org/about/trademark, read 2026-11-24) grants an automatic license for uses that exclusively foster Drupal. It requires a license for, among others, use that suggests an official link (I.B.1), and use in a title or product name that is not exclusively for fostering Drupal and does not also name the user's own organization (I.B.6). Its rules of use ask that the company name appear with the mark (III.4, III.5) and that the mark carry the notice "Drupal is a registered trademark of Dries Buytaert." (III.6). The policy covers the official logo, which may be shown standalone and unaltered; the Drupal icon (the drop) is "subject to separate licensing". The listing is named "Drupal CMS by Tech Progress", naming the publisher next to the mark as the rules of use ask, and its icon is the unaltered "Drupal CMS" logo (drop plus wordmark) that Drupal CMS 2.2.10 ships in its installer theme, not the drop alone. The README and the marketplace copy say that this is a community template, not affiliated with or endorsed by Dries Buytaert or the Drupal Association, and carry the notice.
